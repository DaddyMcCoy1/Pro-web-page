Our Story

From a thought to a reality

What started as a simple thought has grown into a passion driven agricultural business rooted in Edo State, Nigeria with a contact area around Ibeju Lekki, Lagos State, nigeria.

With a vision to contribute to food production and promote local agriculture, we have established an oil palm farm and expanded into the production and supply of quality palm oil, garri, egusi and other agricultural produce. Our Commitment is to quality, freshness, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.

We are proud of how far we have come and remain dedicated to growing, producing, and feeding communities.

From our farm to your table, quality you can trust!

Our Services

Wholesale and Retail of Farm produced palm oil

We produce and supply quality farm produced palm oil, carefully processed to deliver freshness and value for homes, retailers, restuarants, distributors in any size starting from 100ml to 25litres.

Garri Production - Yellow and White

We process quality cassava into delicious yellow and white garri, ensuring good taste and quality for everyday consumption.

Prodution of Egusi and other farm products

We process and supply quality egusi (melon seeds), carefully prepared for household consumption, retailers and bulk buyers. Also other farm produces like ogbono, Pumpkin leaves, palm kernel among others.

Packaging for souvenirs and gifts for events

We have special packages that include jotters, and a customized bag for your events should incase you want to have the palm oil shared as a souvenir at your event.

Whether you are buying for personal use or commercial purposes, we are your reliable source for quality farm produce at competitive prices.
